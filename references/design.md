# ESOC design

This is a reusable print style based on the supplied ESOC website and logo references. It is not a claim that ESOC has approved a new official brand manual.

## Type

Source Sans 3 Regular for text and control numbers; Semibold for course codes and short headings. Use the bundled static TTF files. The Google Fonts version and Adobe's static files share the same family. Keep the font licence with the files.

Map numbers: 13 pt at the example's print scale. Adjust to the selected map specification and print scale; keep labels upright and away from their features. Body text: 10 pt. Small print: 7 pt. Course code: 15 pt. Host title: 17 pt. No decorative fonts or all-capital slogans.

## Colour

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| ESOC green | `#006B00` |
| ESOC red | `#E60000` |
| Light fill | `#F2F7ED` |
| Text | `#231F20` |
| Fine rule | `#CAD8C7` |
| Course overprint | `#A00082` |

The first four colours come from the official logo SVG. The rule colour and course overprint are print-layout choices. Keep course markings purple; do not use club red or green for them.

## Shape and space

Map frames are square. A small corner key may have a 1.2 mm radius. Worksheet boxes may use the same radius. Avoid pill-shaped headings, shadows, gradients and large colour panels. Crop to useful terrain around each course. Preserve scale and relevant path connections. Arrange compact maps on the same A4 sheet rather than filling the page with unrelated terrain. Use a fine dotted cutting line between maps.

Preserve the logo's proportions. Use the supplied SVG or PNG; do not redraw it. Keep it small and clear of controls and routes. The lettering inside the logo is part of the logo, not the document font.
